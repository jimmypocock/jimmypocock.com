it's easier now to sift through the bullshit in ai discourse. if your goal is to invest in ai companies and make some money on the swing ups, build it up. feel like you're a part of it. but people talk about it like it thinks. it doesn't think. it constructs language. it's a brilliant technology, but it isn't an engineer. but after using it for hours and hours a day, everyday, to research and write code and help me understand why i'm facing certain technical issues, i'm not in the least sold on its ability to think. i'm sold on its ability to structure language in a way that makes sense to humans. most probably don't care about that distinction. most may not like thinking that way because it seems limiting, and it's nice to believe ai can understand what you're trying to do. but as i've been using it on every new project, the distinction seems more and more important for me to know how to quickly get what i want from it. i can't expect it to share my vision. i can't expect it to give me vision. it can give me ideas, and it can try really hard to structure language in a way that it sounds like it's trying to share my vision, but it's getting easier to see through.
