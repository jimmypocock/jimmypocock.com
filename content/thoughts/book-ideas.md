# A Church in a Small Town

About a community in which exclusivity exists. Difficult for its people to come to terms with reality outside of the walls of the church and with the belief systems outside of what the Bible provides.

Store can progress by telling of multiple characters ostracized by this church's casting away because of a code of morality not based on observable situations or experiences.
