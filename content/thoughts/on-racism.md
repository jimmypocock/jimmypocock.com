In this country, there is a seedy undercurrent of not just racism, but complacency towards racism, and now it is out in the open. Now, those that sit idly by in times of moral crisis, those that suffer from moral abiguity are cast in the same light as those that are morally corrupt. And I say good. Get them out in the open. Let's see their weakness. And when that weakness rears its ugly head, we will call it out and say "we do not accept this, we will not allow this, not in our country." We life for suffering. We live by empathizing and we live to fight, to interact, to make war so there can be peace, and we will have peace. At any cost, we will have peace.
